# Arrow

A planned curation experiment at Octant. It is set for early 2027. Arrow tests one part of the Decentralized Curation design. It does not realize the full design, and it is not the name of our work.

## What It Tests

Invited experts score real work against a published policy. In the framework, scoring against a published policy, rather than ruling a claim true or false, is how relevance is assessed.

## What It Leaves Out

In the full design, curators stake to take part and are drawn by lottery. Arrow's jurors are invited experts instead. Arrow assumes they are honest and competent and do not collude, so it cannot show what happens when they are not, and it does not validate the full permissionless design.
