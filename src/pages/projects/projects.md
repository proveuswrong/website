# Projects

The Decentralized Curation framework, its applications, and an experiment at Octant that tests one part of it.

## The Framework

### [Decentralized Curation](/projects/decentralized-curation/)

A general framework for curating information, by our founder Ferit Tunçer. It proposes open rules designed to reward people for producing, assessing and correcting information, so that no single actor has to be trusted. Preprint, 2026.

## Applications

An application takes the framework into one information domain and sets its rules there. The framework paper also sketches advertising.

### [News: The Truth Post](/projects/the-truth-post/)

The news application, and the one information domain the framework paper works out in full. A partial version, the accuracy layer only, went live on Ethereum mainnet in June 2023.

### [Public Goods Funding](/projects/public-goods/)

Assessing public goods work in the open, and funding it by the assessment. A proposed design in its own preprint, October 2026; not a validated deployment.

## Experiments

An experiment tests one part of a design under controlled conditions. It does not realize the full design.

### [Arrow](/projects/arrow/)

A planned curation experiment at Octant. [[CONFIRM: It is set for early 2027.]] Invited experts score real work against a published policy. Arrow is listed here because it tests one part of the Decentralized Curation design; it is an experiment, not a product.
